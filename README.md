## 🌐 Website Architecture & Pages
This is a 100% static, fast-loading, responsive multi-page website with zero database dependencies:

1. **Home (`index.html`)** — Focused landing page with hero banner, intro snippet, product previews, service previews, online order CTA, and location preview.
2. **About Us (`about.html`)** — Detailed background, community healthcare approach, proprietor profile, supporting firm, and actual storefront photo.
3. **Medicines & Products (`medicines.html`)** — 7 core healthcare categories with commercial product photography and WhatsApp availability enquiries:
   - Tablets & Capsules (टैबलेट और कैप्सूल)
   - Syrups (सिरप)
   - General Medicines (सामान्य दवाइयां)
   - First Aid (प्राथमिक उपचार सामग्री)
   - Healthcare Products (हेल्थकेयर प्रोडक्ट्स)
   - Personal Care (पर्सनल केयर)
   - Medical Essentials (जरूरी मेडिकल सामग्री)
4. **Our Services (`services.html`)** — 5 verified healthcare assistance services and compliance guidelines.
5. **Online Order (`online-order.html`)** — 4-step WhatsApp ordering guide with Schedule H/H1 prescription compliance notice.
6. **Contact Us (`contact.html`)** — Full address, Google Maps directions, helpline links, and WhatsApp enquiry form.
7. **Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.html`)** — Transparent privacy policy regarding customer communication.
8. **Terms & Conditions (`terms-and-conditions.html`)** — Comprehensive terms, DPCO pricing disclaimers, and legal jurisdiction (Azamgarh, UP).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5:** Semantic, accessible markup.
- **CSS3:** Custom healthcare design system with emerald green & clinical blue accents, mobile-first responsive layout.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** Mobile drawer toggle, dynamic active navigation, and instant WhatsApp message generator.
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Hind`, `Noto Sans Devanagari`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`).
- **Icons:** Font Awesome 6.

## 🚀 Local Development / Preview
Serve with any static HTTP server:
```bash
# Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8088

# Node.js
npx serve .
```
Visit `http://localhost:8088` in your browser.
